CURRENT PHASE

Paper Validation

TrevorTrader is currently validating two SPY Panic Dip Buy strategies. Both are designed to identify sharp intraday SPY declines and wait for evidence that the selloff may be reversing before entering a bullish options position.

Current focus: Compare two different confirmation approaches to determine which method identifies higher-quality reversals while avoiding false starts. Every TRADE and NO TRADE decision is captured and reviewed through controlled paper testing.

What comes next

  1. Paper execution foundationAutomated runners, risk controls, Trade Memory, reconciliation, and Daily Review.
  2. Panic Dip Buy validationPaper-test and compare our two SPY Panic Dip Buy strategies across different market conditions.
  3. Check + learnReview TRADE and NO TRADE decisions, execution quality, market context, and Life of Every Trade™.
  4. Improve + revalidateTurn evidence into testable improvements, then backtest and paper-test before changing the strategies.
