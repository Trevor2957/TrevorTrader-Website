PDCA Research Lab

Every change is tested before it counts.

Each idea follows Plan, Do, Check, Act and our research steps: propose, test, audit, shadow, validate, then promote. An idea becomes a numbered marker on the performance chart only after the new code is verified as active.

  1. Proposed Idea written down, not tested
  2. Testing In offline test, audit or shadow
  3. Implemented Running in paper trading; code hash, activation time, build id and first trade verified; still being judged
  4. Validated Met its pre-agreed bars on 100+ new trades, with uncertainty shown
  5. Rejected Did not meet its bars; record kept

Active PDCA record

PDCA #001Testing

10-Minute Profit Cooldown · SURGE Simple 3

After a fully closed winning trade, no new buy in the same stock for 10 minutes.

Hypothesis
Blocking quick re-entries after a win avoids losing trades. Not proven.
Implementation verification
Offline tests and code audit complete. Activation in paper trading: pending verification.
Performance evaluation
Not started. Interim checkpoint at 40 trades or 4 weeks (progress only, not proof). First validation assessment at 100 or more new trades, with an uncertainty range, against the preserved October 13–14 baseline.
Measurement
Win rate, average win and loss, gross and net P&L, profit factor (screen ≥1.2 after costs), drawdown ($200 guardrail and risk-adjusted), and each entry signal the cooldown blocked with what it would have done. See the before and after table.

FI-01Proposed · not yet tested

Scanner optimization

Find stronger momentum stocks earlier.

Hypothesis
A scanner that ranks earlier and stronger movers may give better entries than today's price, gain and volume filters.
Proposed measurement
Minutes between a stock's first +10% move and the scanner picking it; best move after the pick (MFE); stocks caught vs. missed, replayed against the current scanner.
Results
None yet. Not tested.
FI-02Proposed · not yet tested

Entry optimization

Improve breakout timing and avoid late entries.

Hypothesis
Entering closer to the breakout trigger may cut losing trades that start in the red.
Proposed measurement
Fill price vs. trigger price; share of entries classed too early, right moment, too late or false breakout; worst move in the first minute (MAE); win rate.
Results
None yet. Not tested.
FI-03Proposed · not yet tested

Level II integration

Evaluate order-book information.

Hypothesis
Bid and ask depth at the moment of a signal may help separate breakouts that hold from ones that fail.
Proposed measurement
Whether order-book features at signal time differ between winning and losing trades in a replay. Needs an order-book data feed, which TrevorTrader does not have yet.
Results
None yet. Not tested.

Identify better trading windows.

Hypothesis
Results may differ by time of day, so some windows may be worth avoiding.
Proposed measurement
Trades, win rate, average win and loss, and profit factor for each 30-minute window.
Results
None yet. Not tested.
FI-05Proposed · not yet tested

Lock in Green

Test protecting unrealized profits.

Hypothesis
Moving the stop up once a trade is ahead may keep gains that would otherwise turn into losses.
Proposed measurement

Results
None yet. Not tested.
