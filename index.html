BUILT FOR THE FUTURE

From trading system to software platform.

TrevorTrader is being engineered as a modular platform, not a collection of disconnected bots. Strategies can remain independent while sharing disciplined execution, portfolio-level risk controls, reconciliation, Trade Memory, monitoring, and review.

01 · MODULAR

Independent strategies

Each strategy keeps its own rules, evidence, version history, and performance record.

02 · CONTROLLED

Risk before execution

Orders must pass centralized validation and risk controls before they can reach the broker.

03 · TRACEABLE

Every decision has a record

Signals, orders, outcomes, rejected decisions, and reconciliation events are designed to remain auditable.

04 · LEARNING

Evidence before promotion

Potential improvements are measured, backtested, and paper-validated before they can change production behavior.

Future product direction: TrevorTrader is being built with a commercial-quality architecture in mind. It is not currently offered for sale. Current work remains focused on development, paper trading, validation, and proving the system before any future customer release.
