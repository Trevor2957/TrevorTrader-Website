Life of the Trade™

A simple evidence trail for every paper trade: what triggered the setup, when the system entered, how it managed the position, where it exited, and what the reconciled record teaches us.

PAPER VALIDATION
1SignalThe CORE methodology identifies a potential setup.
2ConfirmThe setup must satisfy its confirmation requirements before entry.
3EntryRisk controls pass and the broker confirms the actual fill.
4ManageThe assigned CORE exit variant manages the open position.
5Exit + ReviewThe broker-confirmed exit is reconciled and reviewed against what happened next.
One CORE entry. Controlled exit experiments.

The current research keeps entry methodology consistent across three paper variants and changes the exit objective. That makes the comparison easier to interpret and keeps strategy development focused.

REALIZED

What the trade earned or lost

PATH

What happened while held

The review preserves the trade path so favorable and adverse movement can be studied.

CONTEXT

What happened after exit

Post-exit movement is useful research context, but it is never counted as realized performance.
